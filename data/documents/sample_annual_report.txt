ABC CORP — ANNUAL REPORT 2025
==============================

Page 1: Letter to Shareholders
-------------------------------
Fiscal year 2025 was a year of continued top-line growth for ABC Corp,
with revenue increasing to $13.1 million from $12.4 million in 2024, an
increase of 5.6%. However, profitability was pressured for the second
consecutive year as operating costs rose faster than revenue.

Page 12: Revenue Overview
--------------------------
Revenue grew across all three business segments in 2025. The Enterprise
Solutions segment was the largest contributor, growing 7% year over year
on the back of two new multi-year client contracts signed in Q2 2025.
The Retail Products segment grew 4%, and the Services segment grew 3%.
Management expects this growth trend to continue into 2026, supported by
an expanding sales pipeline.

Page 24: Operating Expenses
-----------------------------
Total operating expenses increased to $11.4 million in 2025, up from
$10.8 million in 2024, an increase of 5.6%. The largest driver was a 9%
increase in employee compensation and benefits following the company's
expanded hiring in the Enterprise Solutions division. Additionally, the
company incurred one-time costs of approximately $250,000 related to the
relocation of its regional office, and marketing spend increased 12% to
support the launch of two new product lines.

Page 31: Profitability Analysis
---------------------------------
Net profit was $1.7 million in 2025, compared to $1.6 million in 2024 and
$2.0 million in 2023. While profit increased slightly year over year in
absolute terms, profit margin declined to 13.0% in 2025 from 12.9% in 2024
and 18.2% in 2023. The decline in margin over the two-year period is
primarily attributable to the increase in operating expenses outpacing
revenue growth, driven by higher headcount costs, one-time relocation
expenses, and increased marketing investment. Management believes these
are largely non-recurring or growth-related investments and expects
margin to stabilize as the newly signed Enterprise Solutions contracts
reach full revenue recognition in 2026.

Page 38: Balance Sheet Highlights
------------------------------------
Total assets grew to $19.1 million at the end of 2025, up from $18.3
million in 2024. Total liabilities increased to $8.0 million from $7.7
million, primarily reflecting a new $500,000 equipment financing
arrangement used to support the Enterprise Solutions division's
infrastructure buildout.

Page 45: Cash Flow
--------------------
Operating cash flow was $1.6 million in 2025, compared to $1.45 million
in 2024. The improvement reflects the increase in net profit and tighter
receivables collection, partially offset by higher capital expenditure
related to the office relocation.

Page 52: Risk Factors
------------------------
The company identifies the following as its most significant financial
risks for the coming year: (1) continued wage inflation in a competitive
hiring market, particularly for engineering talent; (2) customer
concentration risk, with the top three Enterprise Solutions clients
representing approximately 28% of total revenue; (3) rising input costs
for the Retail Products segment due to global supply chain pressures; and
(4) interest rate exposure on the company's variable-rate equipment
financing.

Page 58: Outlook for 2026
-----------------------------
Management expects revenue growth of 8-10% in 2026, driven by full-year
recognition of the Enterprise Solutions contracts signed in 2025. Profit
margin is expected to improve to approximately 14-15% as one-time
relocation costs do not recur and as revenue growth outpaces the planned
increase in operating expenses.
